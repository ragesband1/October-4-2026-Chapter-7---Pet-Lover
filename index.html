But he wanted to buy the pets in the market of “Pet Lover”. Because pets in here are the wise creatures that can understand without break up pets. Or should we say you can’t live the pets in your own life.

Vaki buyed the pet on his own. He buy the buddy with the fur that makes him cuddles on his own skin. And named the pet Danny the dog pet and the dog fighter with just 1 year old dog.
